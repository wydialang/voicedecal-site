## Grading

The course can only be taken with the P/NP grading option. A minimum of 70% is required to pass the course. The grade breakdown is as follows:

| Component | Weight |
|:----------|:--------|
| Attendance | 50% |
| Homework | 25% |
| Weekly quizzes | 5% |
| Final project | 20% |

### Attendance
Due to the interactive nature of the course, attending lectures is required for students to fully learn/engage with the material. Attendance is mandatory, and every unexcused absence will result in a **10% grade deduction**. Students who need to request an excused absence due to extenuating circumstances can fill out the [excused absence form](). 

### Late work
Students can request extensions of up to 7 days by filling out the [extension request form](). Extensions beyond this point may be handled on a case-by-case basis through communication with the instructor. Unexcused late submissions will not receive credit/instructor feedback. 

### Extra Credit
Students can earn extra credit through participation in the class Discord server. Examples of participation include sharing or analyzing recordings/examples of voices (or any other interesting voice-related media), as well as providing constructive feedback.

## Academic integrity
Written reflections must be your own work. Please cite any sources or
recordings you discuss. Standard [UC Berkeley academic integrity
policies](https://teaching.berkeley.edu/berkeley-honor-code) apply.
